# Research log

Append-only. One entry per ingest batch, connection pass, notes pass, or
restructure (moves, renames, retags). Newest last.

Format:

```
### YYYY-MM-DD — <workflow>: <one-line scope>
- created: <paths>
- updated: <paths> (<what changed>)
- restructure: <before> → <after>
- open: <anything left for the user>
```
